01 / ABOUT

A little about me.

My academic interests lie at the intersection of ultrasound imaging, signal processing, mathematical optimization, and machine learning.

I am pursuing my PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Concordia University, where I am part of the IMPACT Lab led by Prof. Hassan Rivaz. My previous research at BUET focused on multimodal breast-ultrasound analysis and explainable AI.

This website shares my published research, completed contributions, and academic milestones. It is updated as new work becomes public.
